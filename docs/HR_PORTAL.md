# HR portal (`/hr`)

HR staff sign in with a **`KZH####` user ID and password**. There are two roles:
* `hr_admin`: everything, including creating other administrators.
* `hr`: everything except creating or resetting administrators.

The sidebar badges show work that needs attention: candidates awaiting a decision, onboarding forms pending, leave waiting on HR, open requests, and reviews due.

## Overview

**Dashboard** shows:
* headcount (probation, notice, joined in the last 30 days, by department) and 12-month attrition
* today's check-ins and late arrivals
* pending leave and HR requests, candidates awaiting a decision, onboarding forms pending
* the hiring funnel and open roles (applicants and hired against openings)
* who is on leave today
* probation ends, birthdays and work anniversaries in the next 30 days
* recent hires

## Recruitment

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Hiring pipeline** | Board columns: resume screen, mock test, interview, decision, selected and hired. Filter by role or search. A candidate card opens a drawer with the decision panel (Select / Not selected), competency scorecard, evidence-based SWOT, integrity signals, ATS breakdown, test topic results, interview summary and full transcript (including code), emails sent, timeline and resume download |
| **Job openings** | Create and edit openings (title, department, location, minimum years, CTC, openings, required skills, description), and open or close them |
| **Benchmarking** | Rank all candidates for one role on the same criteria, with each competency compared to the role average |
| **Onboarding** | Each selected candidate: waiting for the form, or completed with Employee ID and joining date |

## People

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Employees** | Directory with search and department/status filters. **Add employee** for existing staff. The profile has seven tabs: **Overview** (personal, bank, skills, skill gap, education, experience), **Job & access** (designation, department, reporting manager, status, joining/probation/exit dates, location; reset password or deactivate the account; direct reports), **Leave** (balances, history, approve/reject, attendance calendar), **Payroll** (CTC, monthly TDS, payslips), **Requests**, **Performance** (reviews, add review), **Documents** (generate certificates and letters) |
| **Leave** | The approval queue for all leave. Requests without a manager wait here; HR can decide any request. Edit leave types (quota, notice, maximum consecutive days, paid, half days, probation). Manage holidays |
| **Attendance** | Day view per employee: status, check-in and check-out. Pick any date |
| **Payroll** | Run payroll for a month: every active employee, pro-rated for joining date and unpaid leave, with PF, PT and TDS. Payslip list with totals. Salary structures (click through to edit CTC/TDS) |
| **Service desk** | Every employee request in one queue. Filter by status and type. Open a request to reply, mark it in progress, or approve/resolve or reject. Approving has effects: an attendance correction updates attendance, a document request generates the document, a resignation moves the employee to notice with an exit date |
| **Policies** | Write and edit company policies (one rule per line works best). Employees read them and the AI assistant answers from them |

## Intelligence

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Talent & skills** | **Find people inside Kairoz first**: search employees by required skills (or an opening's skills). Results are split into *ready now* and *could be ready with training*, with training suggestions. Also shows the company-wide skill map and skills by department |
| **Performance** | Check-ins due at 3/6/12 months. **Recruitment intelligence**: how each recruitment signal correlates with later performance ratings |

## Admin

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Access & accounts** | Every account (HR and employees): role, status (active, temporary password, locked, deactivated), last sign-in. Add HR users (emails a `KZH` ID), reset passwords (emails a temporary one and signs the user out everywhere), deactivate or reactivate |
| **Email log** | Every email the system sent, with delivery status and errors |
| **Audit log** | Sign-ins, profile views (which expose PAN and bank details), salary changes, approvals, account changes, onboarding |
